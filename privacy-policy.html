Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy — TADa for YouTube

Last Updated: October 14, 2026 • Developer: Nguyen Tuan Anh (TADa Team)
🛡️ Zero Data Collection Policy: TADa for YouTube runs 100% locally on your computer. We do not track, collect, store, or sell any personal data or browsing history to remote servers or third parties.

1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing TADa for YouTube ("the Extension"), developed by Nguyen Tuan Anh as part of the TADa Team. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details our data practices and demonstrates full compliance with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Policies.

2. Data Collection & Usage Practices

A. Personal Information

We do not collect names, emails, addresses, credit cards, passwords, IP addresses, or government identifiers.

B. Browsing History & Analytics

We do not monitor, log, or transmit your browsing history, visited web pages, video views, search queries, or user interactions.

C. Local Device Storage

The Extension exclusively uses chrome.storage.local inside your browser to save user preferences locally on your machine:

  • Audio & Display Settings: Volume boost level (up to 500%), Ambient Light spread & brightness.
  • Feature Toggles: On/off states for ad-blocking, PiP button, Screenshot button, A-B looper, custom speed.
  • Video Bookmarks & Notes: Timestamps and personal notes that you save for videos. Stored only in your local browser storage.
  • Blocked Ad Counter: Local numeric counter showing how many ad requests were filtered.

3. Explanation of Requested Permissions

Permission Technical Justification
declarativeNetRequest Blocks intrusive video advertisements, trackers, and telemetry requests on YouTube using local static rules. No network data is inspected or transmitted.
activeTab Grants temporary, user-initiated communication with the active YouTube tab when interacting with the extension popup to apply settings instantly.
storage Persists feature toggles, volume multiplier, and video bookmarks locally inside your browser via chrome.storage.local.
Host Permissions (*://*.youtube.com/*) Injects player controls (Volume slider, Ambient light canvas, PiP, Screenshot, A-B Looper) directly onto YouTube video watch pages.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Sales

We strictly adhere to the Chrome Web Store Limited Use Policy:

  • We never sell, rent, or monetize user data.
  • We do not transfer user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • We do not determine creditworthiness, lending eligibility, or perform profiling.

5. Remote Code Policy

In accordance with Manifest V3 policies, all extension code (JavaScript, CSS, HTML, icons) is packaged directly within the installation file. No external scripts, remote CDNs, or eval() calls are used.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You can delete all stored settings and bookmarks at any time by clicking "Xóa hết" inside the Bookmarks modal or by uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions/.
